FAQs

Questions, answered.

What it costs, how much of your team's time it takes, and what I will and won't do.

Working together

Who do you work with?

Independent creative agencies and studios across design, branding and marketing. Usually founders, creative directors and studio managers whose teams have grown faster than the way they work.

Who will I actually be working with?

Me. You'll work directly with me from our first conversation to the final handover. No account managers, no rotating consultants and no getting passed between departments.

Will you work directly with our team?

Yes, depending on the service. The best improvements come from understanding the people doing the work, so team interviews and facilitated workshops are part of the Workflow Audit and The Creative Reset.

Will you manage our projects or team?

No. My role is to improve how your team works, not to take over its day-to-day management. I'll help you set up clearer workflows, responsibilities and planning practices your team can keep using on its own.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Calls and workshops usually run online over Google Meet, so I can work with agencies wherever they're based. In-person sessions are also an option, and we can discuss them when we agree the scope.

Will what we share stay confidential?

Yes. What you tell me about your agency, your team and your clients stays between us.

Services

Which service is right for us?

If you have one specific problem, start with The Clarity Call. If you know something isn't working but can't pin it down, The Workflow Audit finds it. If you're ready to change how a part of your studio runs, The Creative Reset redesigns it with your team. Not sure? That's what the free discovery call is for.

How much of our team's time will it take?

It depends on the service. The Clarity Call is a short questionnaire and a 90-minute call. The Workflow Audit needs a 60-minute leadership session, three 30-minute team interviews and a findings session. The Creative Reset involves more of your team, through interviews and one or two workshops over four to six weeks.

Will we need to change our project management software?

Not necessarily. I'll look at how your existing tools support your workflows and suggest improvements where they help. Software setup, migrations and technical work aren't part of my services.

Do you rebuild platforms or build automations?

No. I focus on processes and people: briefing, allocation, feedback, approvals and handovers. I'll help you use the tools you already have better, but platform rebuilds, migrations, integrations and automations are out of scope.

Can we start small and do more later?

Yes. A Clarity Call or Workflow Audit is a good way to start, and what we find can feed straight into a Creative Reset if you want to go further.

Pricing and booking

How much does it cost?

The Clarity Call is £195. The Workflow Audit starts from £1,100 and The Creative Reset from £3,995. The final price for the Audit and the Reset depends on your team and the scope we agree, and you'll know it before anything starts.

Is the discovery call really free?

Yes. It's 20 minutes, with no obligation and no sales pitch. We'll talk about what's going on in your studio and whether I can help.

How do I book a call?

Click any Book a Discovery Call button, pick a time that suits you and answer a few quick questions. You'll get a calendar invite with a Google Meet link straight away.

What happens after the discovery call?

If there's a good fit, we'll agree which service makes sense, what it covers and the timings before anything starts. If I'm not the right fit, I'll tell you.
