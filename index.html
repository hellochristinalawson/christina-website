How we'll work

Honest conversations that mix the practical with the personal.

Every mentoring relationship looks a little different, because every person does. Some sessions are about strategy and next steps. Others are about what's going on underneath: your values, your confidence, and the story you tell yourself about work.

  • Honest conversations

    A space to say what you really think about your work, your ambitions and the people around you.

  • Your values at the centre

    We'll get clear on what matters to you, so the decisions you make feel like your own.

  • The practical and the personal

    Skills and strategy, alongside the confidence and habits that sit underneath them.

  • Actionable next steps

    You'll leave every session with clear, practical actions to work on, so your progress keeps building between our conversations.

Along the way, I'll bring in tools when they're useful, like my PRISM framework or an optional Human Design reflection. You'll find them in the Development Package.

About me

Hello! I'm Christina, and I've been where you are.

I used to be a terrible manager. Nobody taught me how to lead, manage or develop a team, so I learnt everything the hard way. Now, I'm the manager I wish I'd had, and I've helped others become the same.

If you're here, I'm guessing you really care about how you show up in the world. You want to do a good job, make thoughtful decisions and support the people who rely on you. And sometimes, you need a little support yourself.

I know what it's like to balance the needs of your team with expectations from above. Now I'm the person in your corner: an honest sounding board, practical support and a fresh perspective while you turn your goals into real progress.
