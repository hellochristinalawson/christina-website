02

Redesign and introduce · Flagship

The Creative Reset

Give your creative operations a proper reset.

A collaborative engagement to redesign and introduce better ways of working. We focus on one defined workstream, not an agency-wide overhaul.

Duration
4–6 weeks
Best for
Growing agencies ready to improve everyday operations
Investment
Shared on your discovery call
Book a Discovery Call

What we can deliver

We choose two or three, based on the workstream we tackle.

  • Workflow playbookOr a revised map of your project stages
  • Roles and responsibilities matrixIncluding who owns decisions and approvals
  • Resource planning frameworkWith a weekly allocation routine
  • Briefing checklistPlus intake and prioritisation guidelines
  • Communication and feedback rulesIncluding meeting and escalation routines
  • Team workshopsOne or two facilitated sessions and a concise handover guide

Not includedSoftware changes, daily scheduling and ongoing management stay with your team.

How the six weeks run

  1. Week 1

    Discover

    Interviews, a current-state workflow map and the baseline issues.

  2. Weeks 2–3

    Redesign

    Co-design clearer briefing, allocation, handover, approval or communication practices.

  3. Weeks 4–5

    Introduce

    Facilitated workshops, testing the new practices on real scenarios and gathering feedback.

  4. Week 6

    Refine and hand over

    Adjust the processes, document ownership and set up ways to monitor results.

Each month

Example monthly priorities

Not includedDaily resource allocation, line management, direct client management or unlimited support.

What's included

Processes and people, not software builds

In scope

  • Workflow mapping and redesign
  • Capacity planning practices
  • Team roles and decision frameworks
  • Briefing, handovers and approvals
  • Workshops and adoption support
  • Leadership guidance
  • Reviewing how you use your existing tools

Out of scope

  • Software installation or migration

  • Acting as a line manager

  • Employment-law or formal HR advice
  • Technical integrations or development
